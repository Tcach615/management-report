 Here is a comprehensive research overview of **IT-Koncept SA**:

## 1. Origin & Company History

IT-Koncept SA is a **Swiss IT services company founded in 2013** . It is registered in the Swiss Commercial Register under number **CHE-229.451.519** and is part of the **Business-Koncept group** .

- **Original headquarters**: Mies (Vaud)
- **Current headquarters**: Route du Stand 68, 1260 Nyon, Switzerland (moved in September 2023) 
- The company celebrated its **10-year anniversary in March 2024** 
- **Share capital**: CHF 500,000 (increased in January 2026)

## 4. Target Audience

IT-Koncept primarily targets:

- **Swiss SMEs (PME)** in the Romandy region (French-speaking Switzerland) — Nyon, Geneva, Vaud 
- **Both private and public organizations** undergoing digital transformation 
- Companies seeking **end-to-end IT partners** rather than multiple vendors

Notable clients include: Genève-Servette Hockey Club, ArsPanis Suisse SA (Panino Giusto), Securelec, Areas Genève, CFH, Swisscom (partner), Migros, Fnac, BBGI Group, Swiss Language Group, and Perrin Holding .

## 6. Departments & Service Lines

IT-Koncept operates through several specialized departments/service lines:

| Department | Description |

| **IT Consulting & Audits** | Partial or complete IT audits, organizational studies, IT strategy building  |
